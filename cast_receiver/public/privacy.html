ChordPro Presenter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ChordPro Presenter shows song lyrics and chords on a screen or projector, in one or two languages. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android, on the desktop and on the web.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your songs, setlists and settings are stored on your own device. The developer never receives them.

Information stored on your device

Your songs, setlists, settings and backups are saved on your device. They stay there unless you choose to export, share or back them up. Uninstalling the app removes them.

When the app uses the internet

The app only connects to other services when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Translation: when the app makes a translated copy of a song (the second language), the song's text is sent to Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com) and the translation is returned to the app. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Cloud backup (optional): if you set up a WebDAV backup (for example Koofr or Nextcloud), your songs are sent to the server you chose, using the user name and password you entered. These are stored only on your device and are sent only to that server.
  • YouTube videos: if you add a YouTube video to a song, it plays through YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's privacy policy applies.
  • Song websites: if you search or open a song website from inside the app (for example a chord site), that website loads like it would in a browser, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Casting: when you cast to a Chromecast or TV, the slides are sent over your network to that device. The cast receiver page is loaded from chordpro-presenter.web.app and does not store anything.
  • Band Sync: when you share a set with other devices, the songs travel directly between devices on your local network.

Microphone

The microphone is used only by the built-in tuner, and only while the tuner is open. The sound is analysed on your device to find the pitch. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Sharing with others

The developer does not collect, sell or share your information. Information only leaves your device in the cases above, when you use those features.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Because everything is stored on your device (or on a backup server you chose), you can delete it at any time by deleting songs in the app, clearing the app's data, uninstalling the app, or removing your backup files from your server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
